Chapter 1: The Copper Foundry

The steam whistles of Colombo harbour sounded three times before the brass shutters rolled open. Kalani wiped the grease from her bronze goggles, peering down into the belly of the clockwork assembly.

Every gear inside this device was hand-milled from imported Cornish copper, etched with symbols that predated the arrival of the merchant steamers. The needle inside the manometer flickered with an unnatural pale violet hue.

"You shouldn't tamper with that mechanism," a gruff voice echoed through the foundry. Uncle Jayawardena stepped past the rows of inactive boiler tubes, his wooden cane tapping against the stone floor. "The Harbour Authority has already marked this shipment for impound."

Kalani did not look up. With steady fingers, she inserted the fourth cogwheel into the differential axle. "The Authority does not understand what they are holding. This is not contraband. It is memory."
